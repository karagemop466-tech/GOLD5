Before you buy — check the seller

Check What to do Official source
Do homework on the seller Research the jewelry store or online seller before buying; don't rely solely on what you see online or on social media. FTC — Before You Buy
Independent reviews and complaints Type the seller's name plus the words "complaint" or "review" into a search engine, and see what others say. FTC — Before You Buy
Personal recommendations Ask people you personally know for recommendations. FTC — Before You Buy
Real contact information Verify the business has real contact information (name, address, phone) before purchasing. Checklist requirement (consumer guidance)
If there is a problem Try to resolve with the seller first. If not satisfied, report to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, your state attorney general, or the Jewelers Vigilance Committee (JVC) mediation program. FTC — Report Problems · ReportFraud.ftc.gov · NAAG — State Attorneys General
Limitation: The FTC's official method for checking a seller is search-engine-based (seller name + "complaint"/"review") plus the state attorney general. No government-run jeweler rating database was found. No seller names, ratings, or complaint counts appear on this site (none were verified, none were invented).
