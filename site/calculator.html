Enter a weight and karat to see the metal value. Add an asking price to see the ratio.
fine gold = weight × fineness
metal value = fine gold × spot per gram
price ÷ metal = asking price ÷ metal value

A ratio below 1.00 means the asking price is below the gold value — a fraud signal, not a bargain. A high ratio is normal for diamond rings; it does not by itself mean overpriced. Metal value ignores stones, labour, tax and resale spread.
