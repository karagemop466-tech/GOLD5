The formula used everywhere on this site

fine gold (g) = ring weight (g) × fineness (14K = 0.5833, 18K = 0.75)
metal value (USD) = fine gold (g) × spot (USD per gram)
price ÷ metal value = asking price ÷ metal value
1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 grams

Troy-ounce conversion: NIST SP 811 Appendix B.8 and Kitco's own statement (“A troy ounce is 31.1034768 grams”). NIST ↗

Do not treat this as a valuation of a ringMetal value is a floor check, not an appraisal. A diamond, setting work and brand can legitimately multiply the metal value many times. What metal value is good for: spotting a price that is below the gold itself, which is the classic fraud signal.
