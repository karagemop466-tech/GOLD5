FL-20 Info
needs review
 A 14K or 18K stamp is not proof of purity
FTC guidance requires a karat mark plus a maker's mark, but a stamp is a disclosure, not an assay. The only way to know what the metal actually is: have an independent jeweler test it (XRF or another professional method) after delivery, before wearing or resizing. No US law requires the seller to accept a return based on your test, so confirm the return right first.
