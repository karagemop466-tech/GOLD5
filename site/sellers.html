Seller-specific findings

Blue Nile
Strong policy, mixed reviews
30-day guarantee with clear exclusions (resized/engraved/custom), two returns per year, $150 lost-certificate fee. BBB A+ but 121 complaints in 3 years; ConsumerAffairs 1.7/5. Lifetime warranty only at $1,000+.
Brilliant Earth
Returns stated, policy unavailable
Every product page says “Free Shipping & Returns” and “Free 1-Year Resizing”, but the return-policy URL redirects to the home page. Get the window and exclusions in writing. FL-07
Allurez
Returns verified, exchange-only risk
30 days to arrive, exclusions listed, $300 per missing certificate, buyer pays return shipping. Design Your Own rings are exchange-only — confirm whether a setting-only purchase counts. FL-10
Avianne & Co
Short window
14 days for unworn, unaltered items; a 10% FALL10 code is live. Full policy and resize exclusions were not captured. FL-11
Jewelry Master
30-day guarantee, restrictions unknown
“Some restrictions apply” is all the page says. A $6,041 “retail price” is the seller’s own comparison and is not used in analysis. FL-12
Solid Gold Jewelers
No price, no returns
The page lists “14ky, 4.7g, #B02561” and tells you to call for pricing. No return policy is shown. Get both in writing before sending money. FL-13
Etsy shops
Individually run policies
VeraByEden: 7 days. ForeverForLove and Cittali: 30 days. Three others did not expose a window. Etsy Purchase Protection helps with non-delivery/damage, not change of mind. FL-16
Vetting a new seller
Five-minute check
Search name + “complaint”; look for a BBB profile; call the number; check the address; get the return policy in writing; never pay off-platform. FTC steps ↗
