The 12-point online gold ring checklist

Each point lists its official source and what this project actually found when verifying the listings above. No hallucinations: every claim below is linked; re-run python3 scripts/verify.py after any review.

The key (restated): a 14K or 18K stamp by itself does NOT prove the ring is that purity.

Safest approach: reputable seller with real contact info + proper markings (karat and maker's mark) + reasonable price vs. gold value + return window + credit-card purchase + independent XRF/acid-free test after delivery, before resizing or wearing. If the seller won't let you independently verify or return it: don't buy it.
