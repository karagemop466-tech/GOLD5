The buying checklist · 12 checks

What to demand before you pay

This is the checklist the project was built around, with each point tied to an official source and to what the 25-listing dataset actually shows. Work top to bottom; the last two items are the gate.

The keyA 14K or 18K stamp by itself does not prove the ring is that purity. The safe path is: reputable seller + proper markings + a reasonable price + independent testing after delivery. If the seller will not let you verify or return it, do not buy.
#Check — what to requireOfficial sourceWhat this dataset shows
1 Gold purity (karat)
Confirm 10K, 14K or 18K in the listing text and on the stamp. Higher karat = more gold, but also softer metal.
 FTC — karat marks ↗ All 25 rows state a karat: 23 × 14K, 2 × 18K. A stated karat is a claim, not a test — see the last rows of this checklist.
2 Solid gold — stated explicitly
The listing must say solid gold. Not plated, vermeil, gold-filled, gold overlay, rolled gold plate or gold-tone. “Solid gold” means the item is not hollow.
 FTC — solid vs plated ↗ 9 of 25 listings explicitly use the words solid gold (quoted in the table). The other 16 name a karat but never say solid — recorded as “not stated” and flagged. FL-15
3 Hallmark + maker’s mark
Look for the karat stamp plus the maker’s/manufacturer’s name or registered trademark next to it. Ask for a close-up photo. If the trademark is missing, the FTC says don’t buy.
 FTC · 16 CFR 23.10 · 15 U.S.C. § 297 ↗ 0 of 25 listings state a maker’s mark. 2 claim a karat stamp (no photo). This is the biggest documentation gap in the whole dataset. FL-14
4 Weight in grams
The seller must state the ring’s weight in grams. Without it, neither you nor the calculator can compute gold content. A price alone tells you nothing about how much gold you get.
 16 CFR 23.18 — weight representations ↗ 8 of 25 listings publish a weight. The other 17 are blank and flagged. All published weights are seller claims, not independent measurements. FL-03 · FL-18
5 Seller reputation
Search the business name with “complaint” and “review”. Verify a real phone number and address. Don’t rely on the seller’s own testimonials.
 FTC — before you buy ↗ Only 1 of 13 sellers has independent complaint data captured here (Blue Nile: BBB A+, 121 complaints in 3 years; ConsumerAffairs 1.7/5 from 172 reviews). The rest are blank on the Sellers page — not “clean”, just unchecked. FL-04
6 Price vs. gold value
Compute fine gold and metal value, then compare. A complete ring priced below its metal value is the classic fraud signal. A high markup over metal is normal for diamond rings.
 LBMA benchmark · Kitco spot ↗ 7 rows can be computed. One case-study band prices below metal (0.84×). Setting prices are excluded from that conclusion. Analysis · Calculator
7 Return policy — in writing
Get the window, the condition rules and the exclusions for resized, engraved or custom rings. Resizing can void a return; Blue Nile explicitly excludes items resized after purchase.
 FTC — get return policies in writing ↗ 18 of 25 rows link a return policy that was read and verified (Blue Nile 30 days, Brilliant Earth 30 days at /30-Day-Returns/, Allurez 30 days, plus 3 Etsy shops). Avianne, Jewelry Master and Solid Gold Jewelers do not publish full policy pages; Etsy windows vary — one is 7 days. FL-07 · FL-16
8 Payment protection
Use a credit card. Under federal law you have the right to dispute billing errors, and card networks give you a chargeback route. Avoid wire transfers, Zelle, gift cards and off-platform “deals”.
 FTC — credit cards & disputes ↗ Every seller reviewed checks out by card or Etsy checkout. Etsy Purchase Protection covers non-delivery, damage and material misdescription — not buyer’s remorse. Keep orders inside the platform.
9 Documentation
Save the listing, the receipt, the description, the return policy and every message where the seller claims purity or weight. Screenshot prices: they change.
 FTC — report problems ↗ This repository keeps a dated evidence trail for every row, including the exact quote. Do the same for your purchase: a seller’s written claim is what you can act on later.
10 On arrival — independent jeweler
Before wearing or resizing, take the ring to an independent jeweler and ask them to verify the gold purity and the weight. Resizing can complicate a return.
 Project rule (consumer guidance, not a legal mandate) ↗ No listing here offers a post-delivery verification window. Assume you must complete the check inside the return window, and confirm that in writing before buying.
11 XRF or professional test
Ask for XRF testing or another professional assay. A stamp is a disclosure, not proof. XRF is fast and non-destructive, but it is a service you arrange — no US rule requires the seller to provide it.
 Project rule — no official XRF mandate ↗ No seller in this dataset states that it XRF-tests finished pieces or will accept a return based on your test. That is a gap to close in writing. FL-20
12 If the seller won’t let you verify or return it — don’t buy
A legitimate seller can provide the weight, a hallmark photo, the return terms and a real contact. Refusal is the answer.
 FTC — before you buy ↗ This is a pass/fail gate, not a score. A great price with no return right is not a great price.

Three phrases that are not the same thing

Safe
“Solid 14K gold”
Solid (not hollow), 14K (58.33% gold). Still needs a maker’s mark and an independent test.
Not solid
“14K gold filled / overlay / RGP”
A layer of at least 10K gold mechanically bonded to a base metal. Must be labelled with the fraction if under 1/20 by weight.
Not solid
“14K gold plated / vermeil / tone”
Electroplating over base metal (vermeil = gold over sterling silver). The gold layer wears away. Never treat it as an investment in gold.

Definitions: FTC Consumer Advice, “Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry”; regulation at 16 CFR Part 23.
