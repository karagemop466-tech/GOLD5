# Checklist item What to verify Official source Verification status Notes / irregularities
1 Gold purity — confirm the karat (10K, 14K, 18K, etc.) Karat mark states how much pure gold the piece contains (24-part system; 18K = 18/24 gold, 14K = 14/24 gold) FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry verified —
2 Solid gold Listing explicitly says solid gold; not plated, vermeil, gold-filled, gold overlay, or gold-tone FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry verified —
3 Hallmark + maker's mark 10K/14K/18K stamp plus a maker's/manufacturer's mark; FTC warns: no trademark with the karat mark = don't buy FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry · 16 CFR § 23.10 (National Stamping Act, 15 U.S.C. 297) verified U.S. has no mandatory government assay office; stamp alone is not proof of purity
4 Weight in grams Ring weight in grams provided by seller NIST OWM — Precious Metal Conversion verified Conversion factor verified (1 oz t = 31.1034768 g); specific ring weights are seller-provided, not listed here
5 Seller reputation Independent reviews and complaints; real business contact information FTC — Before You Buy · NAAG — State Attorneys General verified No government-run jeweler rating database found; FTC method is search-engine-based (name + "complaint"/"review")
6 Price vs. gold value Price compared with ring's gold weight and current gold value; unusually cheap = red flag LBMA — Gold Price (benchmark) verified (method) Live spot prices vary by dealer; no product prices are listed or inferred on this site
7 Return policy Return after receiving and inspecting; check exclusions on resized/custom rings FTC — Before You Buy · 16 CFR Part 429 (Cooling-Off Rule) verified (rule scope) No general federal return right for online sales; Cooling-Off Rule covers door-to-door/temporary-location sales only
8 Payment — credit card preferred Credit card for purchase protection; avoid irreversible payment methods FTC — Using Credit Cards and Disputing Charges (FCBA) verified —
9 Documentation Keep listing, receipt, description, and seller's gold-purity claims FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry verified —
10 Independent verification on arrival Independent jeweler verifies purity and weight before wearing/resizing Jewelers of America — Find a Jeweler · FTC — Report Problems (JVC mediation) unverified Guidance from trade association/consumer page; not a codified federal requirement
11 XRF / professional gold-purity test Ask for XRF testing or another professional gold-purity test; don't rely solely on the stamp Thermo Fisher Scientific — XRF testing unverified No government source found mandating/describing consumer XRF testing; industry/vendor source only — flagged
12 Walk away if no verification/return If the seller won't allow independent verification or return, don't buy FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry verified (guidance) —
Reading the status column: verified = confirmed against the linked official source. unverified = could not be confirmed from an official government source; value/claim left as guidance and flagged for review. Nothing on this page was inferred or invented.
