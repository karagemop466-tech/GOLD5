What this is

The goal

Find gold engagement rings that can actually be verified: solid gold (not plated or filled), a stated karat, a hallmark and maker's mark, a published weight in grams, a reputable seller, a price that makes sense against the gold market, a usable return window, and a path to independent XRF testing on arrival.

This dashboard collects every candidate we could verify from an official listing, shows what is known and what is not, and computes the raw-gold value of each ring so an implausible price stands out.

The key finding

A 14K or 18K stamp by itself does not prove the ring is that purity. The FTC's own guidance says a karat mark must sit next to the maker's name or trademark — and if the trademark is missing, don't buy. Not one listing in this dataset shows that combination. The only proof is an independent jeweler's weights and XRF test after delivery, before you wear or resize the ring.
