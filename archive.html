Why keep four sites?

Because the brief for this project says not to discard other sessions' work, and because each archive contains material this dashboard summarises: the docs/ site holds verbatim page excerpts captured at read time, the pages/ site walks each checklist point in more depth, guide/ adds formal citations and research notes, and verified/ adds retailers this dashboard does not price (Ross-Simons, Mejuri, Costco) plus its own independent harness. The dashboard does not duplicate their evidence files — it links to them.

The security checks still cover the archives: python3 tools/check_hallucinations.py audits the docs/ dataset, and python3 scripts/verify.py audits this dashboard and cross-checks the archived links resolve.
