Calculation method: fine-gold grams = net gold-alloy grams × confirmed fineness; metal value = fine-gold grams × dated spot price per gram. These are derived estimates, not purchase prices, resale offers or authentication tests. Gross ring weight including stones must not be treated as net alloy weight.
