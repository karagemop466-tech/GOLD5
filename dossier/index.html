Gold engagement rings — verified buying dossier

A dashboard of gold engagement-ring listings collected against the project's buying checklist. Each entry is stored with its official source link, the UTC moment it was observed, and the verbatim text that proves the number. Blank means "could not be confirmed", not "unknown to us".

⚠ NO HALLUCINATED PRICES — the governing rule of this project

  • Nothing here is estimated, remembered or inferred. Every price, karat claim, metal description and gram weight was read from an official first-party page (the retailer's own product page, the marketplace's own item page, or the benchmark administrator's own site) and stored with three things: the source URL, a UTC capture timestamp, and the literal verbatim excerpt that contains the number.
  • If a value could not be confirmed, the cell is left blank and the row is published on Flagged for review. A blank cell is a deliberate, honest result — never a gap filled by guesswork.
  • Automated anti-hallucination checks run at every build. Current run: 18/18 PASS, 0 FAIL. Check C3 re-parses every published price out of its own evidence excerpt and fails the build if the number is not literally there. Check C6 does the same for gram weights. See Verification.
  • Prices move. Retail jewellery prices and the gold price both change constantly, so every value is a timestamped observation, not a standing quote. Re-read the live page before paying — this dataset contains two documented cases where the same official page was indexed at two different prices (F-05, F-06).

Headline findings

#FindingEvidence
1The big US jewellery chains publish karat but not grams. Kay, Zales and Jared product pages give Metal Type, Metal Colour, Metal Finish and Gold Karat, but no ring weight. The checklist item "a legitimate seller should be able to provide the weight in grams" therefore has to be requested by the buyer — it is not on the page. Official spec blocks captured for KAY-V-992880601, ZAL-V-20050176, JARED-V-993930609
2Marketplace sellers are the ones who publish weights. Every gram weight in this dataset comes from an eBay, Etsy or Amazon listing, not from a chain retailer — and the best of them give the weight in pennyweight and grams, which cross-checks against the NIST conversion. EBAY-126886617045: "Metal Weight: 3.8dwt / 5.90 Grams" (3.8 × 1.55517384 = 5.9097 g ✓)
3"Engagement ring" does not mean solid gold. Pandora operates an official category literally named Gold plated engagement rings, and its own machine-readable product data says "14k Gold-plated Signature metal alloy, No Other Material". us.pandora.net gold-plated engagement rings; PANDORA-162999C01
4The same official page can carry two different prices. Zales product V-15246515 is indexed at $3,999.99 (grade I/I2) and at $3,114.30 (grade J/I2); Pandora's Elevated Heart Ring is $130.00 on its product page and $125.00 on the brand's gold-plated category page. F-05 and F-06 on Flagged for review
5White gold is rhodium-finished — and the retailer says so. Zales states the item is "electroplated with rhodium or finished with an e-coating" and that the finish "may wear off with frequent use". That is a surface finish over a solid gold alloy, not gold plating over base metal, but the wording matters when reading a listing. ZAL-V-20097139 spec block
6Price-to-claim ratio is the strongest fraud signal. An eBay category page shows a "7.50 Ct Oval Cut Lab Grown Diamond Engagement Ring Solid 14K Yellow Gold" at $322.27. The claim and the price cannot both be true.EBAY-BN7024729510-LUX750 — documented as a red flag, not a recommendation
