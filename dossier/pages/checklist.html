The buying checklist, line by line, against official sources

Each checklist item is mapped to the official (or best available) source, to the column in this dataset that evidences it, and to what was actually found. Items that rest on non-official sources are labelled as such rather than dressed up as regulation.

⚠ NO HALLUCINATED PRICES — the governing rule of this project

  • Nothing here is estimated, remembered or inferred. Every price, karat claim, metal description and gram weight was read from an official first-party page (the retailer's own product page, the marketplace's own item page, or the benchmark administrator's own site) and stored with three things: the source URL, a UTC capture timestamp, and the literal verbatim excerpt that contains the number.
  • If a value could not be confirmed, the cell is left blank and the row is published on Flagged for review. A blank cell is a deliberate, honest result — never a gap filled by guesswork.
  • Automated anti-hallucination checks run at every build. Current run: 15/15 PASS, 0 FAIL. Check C3 re-parses every published price out of its own evidence excerpt and fails the build if the number is not literally there. Check C6 does the same for gram weights. See Verification.
  • Prices move. Retail jewellery prices and the gold price both change constantly, so every value is a timestamped observation, not a standing quote. Re-read the live page before paying — this dataset contains two documented cases where the same official page was indexed at two different prices (F-05, F-06).
The key point, stated once: a 14K or 18K stamp by itself does not prove the ring is that purity. U.S. law requires a karat quality mark plus the mark-maker's trademark (15 U.S.C. 297 via 16 CFR §23.10) and makes misrepresenting gold content an unfair or deceptive practice (16 CFR §23.3) — but there is no mandatory pre-sale assay in the United States, unlike the UK hallmarking system. The safe path is a reputable seller + proper markings + a price consistent with the metal + independent testing after delivery.

1Gold purity — confirm the karat
10K, 14K, 18K. Higher karat = more gold.
24K is pure gold; the karat system is 24 parts total, so 18K = 75.0%, 14K = 58.3%, 10K = 41.7% fineness.
  • FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • Jewelers of America — quality marks on karat gold jewellery cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • 16 CFR §23.3 — misrepresentation as to gold content is unfair or deceptive cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
karat column, plus the derived fineness % under it
Every one of the 46 karat assertions in this dataset is checked at build time (C14) to appear verbatim in its own evidence excerpt. Four Brilliant Earth rows had their karat withdrawn because it could only be inferred from a SKU suffix.
2Solid gold — not plated, vermeil, filled, overlay or gold-tone
The listing must explicitly say solid gold.
Gold filled / overlay / rolled gold plate = a layer of at least 10K gold mechanically bonded to base metal. Gold electroplate = at least 10K gold, at least 0.175 microns, electrolytic. Gold flashed/washed = very thin electroplating. Vermeil = sterling silver base plated with gold. Hollow items must not be described as solid gold.
  • FTC — definitions of plated, filled, vermeil, flashed cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • 16 CFR §23.3 cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
The NOT solid gold category (7 rows)
Pandora's own machine-readable product data reads "material":"14k Gold-plated Signature metal alloy, No Other Material", and the brand runs an official category called Gold plated engagement rings. This is the single most important filter in the project.
3Hallmark — karat stamp plus a maker's mark
Look for a 10K/14K/18K stamp AND a manufacturer's mark.
The National Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. 297) requires anyone who applies a quality mark indicating gold or silver content to also apply their trademark or name to the article. The FTC's plain-English warning: if there is no trademark along with the karat mark, don't buy it.
  • 16 CFR §23.10 — quality marks and the National Stamping Act cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • FTC — maker's mark guidance cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
metal_claim column; flags naming missing maker's marks
Only two rows in this dataset evidence a stamp: EBAY-297741882482 ("Hallmark: Stamped '14k' inside the shank") and EBAY-223469269883 ("stamp 14k Z", the Z being the Zales mark). Almost no online listing photographs the hallmark — see F-13.
4Weight — the ring's weight in grams
A legitimate seller should be able to provide it.
1 troy ounce = exactly 31.1034768 g; 1 pennyweight = 1.55517384 g = 0.05 troy oz.
  • NIST Office of Weights and Measures — conversion factors for precious metal sales cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
weight_grams and the derived pure gold (g) column
Only 9 of 58 rows publish a weight, and all of them are marketplace sellers. Kay, Zales, Jared, Blue Nile and Brilliant Earth publish karat, width and finish but no gram weight — you must ask. Weights described as "average" or "gross" are flagged (F-12).
5Seller reputation
Independent reviews and complaints; real contact information.
The FTC's method is practical: search the seller's name plus "complaint" or "review", ask people you know, and report problems to the FTC and your state attorney general.
  • FTC — before you buy cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • ReportFraud.ftc.gov cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • NAAG — state attorney general consumer complaints cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
The Sellers & policies page
Real contact details were captured for Blue Nile (phone, email, WhatsApp, contact page) and Brilliant Earth (phone, contact page, store list). No government-run jeweller rating database exists — flagged in research/seller-reputation.md.
6Price — compare with gold weight and current gold value
An unusually cheap price is a red flag.
The LBMA Gold Price is the benchmark, set twice daily at 10:30 and 15:00 London time and administered by ICE Benchmark Administration.
  • LBMA — precious metal prices live-verified this session
  • ICE Benchmark Administration — LBMA precious metals official source
The calculator and gold price pages
16 observations for 2026-10-22 span $71.50/oz. Two documented cases show the same official page indexed at two different prices (F-05, F-06). The most extreme price-to-claim ratio found is a "7.50 Ct ... Solid 14K Yellow Gold" ring at $322.27 (F-09).
7Return policy
Make sure you can return after inspecting; check resized/custom exclusions.
Get the store's refund and return policies in writing before you buy. The FTC Cooling-Off Rule gives 3 days only for sales at a home, workplace, dorm or the seller's temporary location — it does NOT cover regular retail or most online purchases.

  • FTC — Buyer's Remorse and the Cooling-Off Rule cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • 16 CFR Part 429 cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
return_policy column
Observed: Blue Nile "Free Returns" (live), Kay "Free and easy 30-day returns" (live), Etsy listing "Returns & exchanges accepted" (live), Pandora item marked "FINAL SALE" (no return), one eBay seller offering "Free Resizing" without stating whether a resized ring stays returnable.
8Payment — prefer a credit card
Avoid irreversible payment methods.
The Fair Credit Billing Act sets the dispute process: written dispute within 60 days of the first bill containing the error, issuer acknowledges within 30 days and resolves within two billing cycles (max 90 days), liability for unauthorized charges capped at $50. Note: disputes about the quality of an item are not billing errors under the FCBA.
  • FTC — Using Credit Cards and Disputing Charges cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
Guidance page (no payment data is collected in this dataset)
The FCBA quality-dispute limitation matters here: a ring that assays below its stamped karat is a quality dispute, so chargeback rights are weaker than for non-delivery. That is why post-delivery independent testing and a written return right matter more than the card.
9Documentation
Keep the listing, receipt, description and the seller's purity claims.
Keep the seller's claims; get return policies in writing; a dated receipt or contract should show the seller's name and address.
  • FTC — documentation and report problems cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • 16 CFR §23.10 — the quality mark should remain attached until purchase cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
Every row stores its own evidence.excerpt, URL and UTC timestamp
This dataset is itself the documentation practice applied to research: 58 rows each keep a verbatim excerpt of the seller's claim, which is exactly what the checklist asks a buyer to preserve.
10Upon arrival — independent jeweller before wearing or resizing
Ask them to verify gold purity and weight.
Jewelers of America operates a member directory; the Jewelers Vigilance Committee runs a mediation programme for disputes with jewellers.
  • Jewelers of America — find a jeweller cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • FTC — report problems / JVC mediation cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
Guidance page
Sound consumer practice but not a codified federal requirement — flagged as guidance in research/verification.md.
11Best verification — XRF or another professional purity test
Do not rely solely on the stamp.
X-ray fluorescence is a non-destructive composition test used by jewellers, pawnbrokers, refiners and assay offices. It reads the surface, so plating beneath the surface may not be detected.
  • Thermo Fisher Scientific — how pawnbrokers verify gold authenticity vendor / industry source, not official
  • The Goldsmiths' Company Assay Office description via industry coverage vendor / industry source, not official
Guidance page + F-02
No government source was found mandating or describing XRF for consumers. The available sources are instrument vendors and industry blogs, so this item is published as PARTIALLY VERIFIED and flagged rather than presented as regulation.
12If the seller won't let you verify or return it — don't buy it
The decisive test.
Follows from the FTC guidance to get the return policy in writing before buying and from the National Stamping Act's marking requirement.
  • FTC — before you buy cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
  • 16 CFR §23.10 cited in research/*.md — not re-fetched this session (F-03)
Flags on rows that fail this test
