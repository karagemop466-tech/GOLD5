## Verified facts

| Item | Verified value | Source |
|------|----------------|--------|
| Karat quality mark | Tells how much pure gold the piece contains | FTC consumer page |
| Maker's/trademark mark | Look near the karat mark for the name or U.S. registered trademark of the company that stands behind the mark | FTC consumer page |
| FTC warning | If there is no trademark along with the karat mark, don't buy it | FTC consumer page |
| Quality mark definition | Any mark stamped/embossed/inscribed indicating product is composed of precious metal or alloy | 16 CFR § 23.10 |
| National Stamping Act | Anyone who applies a quality mark indicating gold/silver content must also apply their trademark or name to the article (15 U.S.C. 297) | 16 CFR § 23.10, citing 15 U.S.C. 297 |
| Conspicuousness | Karat designation must be equally conspicuous as the word "gold" | FTC / 16 CFR Part 23 context |

## Plated / filled terms that are NOT solid gold

| Term | FTC description | Source |
|------|-----------------|--------|
| Gold filled / gold overlay / rolled gold plate (RGP) | Layer of at least 10K gold mechanically applied to a base metal; must be marked with karat quality + plating term (e.g., "14K gold overlay") | FTC consumer page |
| Gold electroplate | At least 10K gold, at least 0.175 microns thick, electrolytic process on base metal | FTC consumer page |
| Gold flashed / gold washed | Very thin electroplating; wears away faster | FTC consumer page |
| Vermeil | Sterling silver base coated/plated with gold | FTC consumer page |
| "Solid gold" prohibition on hollow items | Hollow items must not be marked/described as "solid gold" | FTC consumer page; 16 CFR § 23.3 |

## Irregularities / flags

- U.S. hallmarking is NOT government pre-sale assay verification: the FTC requires the mark + the sponsor's trademark, but there is no mandatory independent assay office in the U.S. (unlike the UK hallmarking system). A karat stamp alone does not prove purity. This is stated on the site as a consumer warning and listed on the Flagged for Review page as a limitation to be aware of, not as an error.
