## Verified facts

| Item | Verified guidance | Source |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| Research the seller before buying | Do homework on the seller; don't rely solely on online/social media claims | FTC consumer page |
| Check independent reviews/complaints | Type the seller's name + "complaint" or "review" into a search engine | FTC consumer page |
| Personal recommendations | Ask people you know for recommendations | FTC consumer page |
| Real contact information | Verify the business has real contact information (checklist requirement) | Checklist requirement |
| Report problems | Report to FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and your state attorney general if dissatisfied | FTC consumer page |
| JVC mediation | Jewelers Vigilance Committee operates a mediation program for disputes with jewelers (JVClegal.org) | FTC consumer page |

## Hallucination check

- No specific seller names, ratings, or complaint counts were invented.
- No BBB accreditation claims were made (BBB is a private organization; the FTC's official guidance does not require BBB).

## Irregularities / flags

- The checklist asks to "research independent reviews and complaints." The FTC's official method is search-engine-based (seller name + "complaint"/"review") plus state attorney general. No government-run seller-rating database for jewelers was found; this is noted as a practical limitation, not an error.
