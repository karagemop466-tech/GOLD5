# Return Policy

## Easy Returns

We want you to feel confident about your Allurez purchase. Eligible unused items may be returned within
30 days, subject to the conditions and exceptions below.

### Return eligibility and deadline

**Your return must arrive at our office within 30 days of the original shipment.** Please allow enough time
for return shipping.

- Items must be unused and in their original condition, without scratches or signs of wear. They must not
  have been resized or altered in any way.
[...]
### Exceptions and special conditions

- **Engraved items:** Non-refundable.
- **Design Your Own engagement rings, pendants, and earrings:** May be exchanged for items of the same or
  higher value, but cannot be exchanged for loose diamonds.
- **Custom-made orders:** Non-refundable and non-cancellable.
- **Contoured wedding bands:** Not returnable. [...]
- **Clearance items:** Non-refundable.
- **Appraisals:** Non-refundable.
- **Gift cards:** Not returnable.
- **Layaway deposits:** Non-refundable.

### Shipping costs

You are responsible for return shipping costs. Our original shipping cost is not refundable, including when
your order qualified for free shipping.

### How to return an item

1. **Request your return authorization.** Email [service@allurez.com](mailto:service@allurez.com) or call
   [1-800-554-3509](tel:+18005543509) to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number. [...]
[...]
### Inspection and refunds

Once we receive your return, our quality assurance team will inspect the item. After approval, we will issue
your refund within 7 business days using your original payment method. Payments made by wire transfer will be
refunded by company check.
