## R01 — FTC jewelry buying guidance

- Type / result: `reference` / `unchanged`
- Official URL: <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-platinum-gold-and-silver-jewelry>
- Source excerpt: Near the karat quality mark, look for the name or US registered trademark of the company behind it; if there is no trademark with the karat mark, FTC says do not buy. Solid gold means not hollow. Return policies should be obtained in writing.

## R03 — 15 USC 295 gold fineness tolerance

- Type / result: `reference` / `unchanged`
- Official URL: <https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/15/295>
- Source excerpt: Actual fineness may not be more than three one-thousandth parts below the mark on the tested solder-free part; for the entire article including solder, seven one-thousandth parts applies to articles other than watchcases or flatware.

## R04 — 16 CFR 23.3 gold-content representations

- Type / result: `reference` / `unchanged`
- Official URL: <https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/23.3>
- Source excerpt: Misrepresenting the presence, quantity or karat fineness of gold is unfair or deceptive. The rule also says hollow-center products should not be described as solid gold.

## R05 — 15 USC 297 identifying trademark

- Type / result: `reference` / `unchanged`
- Official URL: <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/297>
- Source excerpt: Section 297(b) requires the identifying trademark or name to be applied by the same means, at least as large, and as close as possible to the quality mark.
