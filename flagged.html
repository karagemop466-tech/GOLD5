F-3 U.S. hallmarking is not government assay The FTC requires the karat mark plus the sponsor's trademark, but there is no mandatory independent government assay office in the U.S. A karat stamp alone is not proof of purity. FTC consumer page; 16 CFR § 23.10 / 15 U.S.C. 297 Noted as a consumer limitation throughout the site
F-4 No government-run jeweler rating database Seller reputation checks rely on FTC's search-engine method (name + "complaint"/"review") plus state attorney general. FTC consumer page; NAAG Method documented; no seller names/ratings listed
F-5 No general federal return right for online sales Online jewelry returns depend on the seller's written policy; the FTC Cooling-Off Rule covers door-to-door/temporary-location sales only. FTC Cooling-Off Rule; 16 CFR Part 429 Stated explicitly on the Return Policy page
F-6 Live gold price Gold prices change constantly. No "current" spot price is claimed on this site; a build-time reference (if shown) is labeled with source and time and is not a live quote. LBMA (benchmark source) Calculator requires user-entered price; reference labeled clearly
