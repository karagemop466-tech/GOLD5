high  F1 — Price is BELOW the computed gold metal value (classic fraud signal)

Case study etsy-576343849: a 7 g '14K solid' band listed at $444.80. At the verified pass-2 Kitco spot, 7 g x 58.33% = 4.083 g of pure gold, which at the verified spot is worth more than the asking price. The price is ~84% of melt value. No legitimate jeweler sells solid gold below melt. The listing is now unavailable — retained as an example of the check.

info  F11 — A 14K/18K stamp alone does not prove purity

FTC: 'Near the karat quality mark, look for the name or the U.S. registered trademark of the company that will stand behind the mark... If you're thinking about buying a piece of gold jewelry but don't see a trademark along with the karat mark, don't buy it.' The stamp is a claim; independent testing (XRF) after delivery is the proof.

Observed at: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-platinum-gold-and-silver-jewelry  ·  Rows: —  ·  Status: open
