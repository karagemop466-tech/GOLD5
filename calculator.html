Fine-gold equivalent = gold-alloy weight × karat ÷ 24
Metal-value proxy = fine-gold equivalent × sourced spot price per fine-gold gram

The K ÷ 24 calculation is a transparent arithmetic conversion on the karat scale, not an assay. The eCFR Jewelry Guides, § 23.3 ↗ address representations about gold content and give examples such as 14-karat gold; they do not verify a particular ring.

How to interpret it

This is a simplified metal-content calculation. It assumes the documented alloy mass and karat are accurate and does not price craftsmanship, gemstones, brand, taxes, shipping, retailer margin, or resale costs. It is not an appraisal, not a buying recommendation, and not proof that a stamped or advertised karat is correct. Ask an independent jeweler to professionally test the received ring and measure it before wearing or resizing.
