Before you buy — check the return policy

Check What to do / what the rule says Official source
Get it in writing Find the store's refund and return policies — and get them in writing — before you buy. FTC — Before You Buy
Return after inspection Make sure you can return the ring after receiving and inspecting it. Checklist requirement (consumer guidance)
Custom / resized rings Check specifically for exclusions on resized or custom rings. Many sellers do not accept returns of sized or custom-made jewelry. Checklist requirement (consumer guidance)
FTC Cooling-Off Rule Gives 3 days to cancel certain sales made at your home, workplace, dormitory, or at a seller's temporary location (hotel, convention center, fairground, restaurant). FTC — Cooling-Off Rule · 16 CFR Part 429
Not covered The Cooling-Off Rule does not apply to regular retail store purchases or most online purchases. Online jewelry purchases depend on the seller's written return policy. FTC — Cooling-Off Rule
If you cancel (covered sales) Cancel until midnight of the third business day after the sale; the seller must refund all payments within 10 business days. FTC — Cooling-Off Rule · 16 CFR Part 429
No federal return right for most online jewelry. There is no general federal rule requiring online sellers to accept returns. The seller's written return policy — including any resized/custom-ring exclusions — is what matters. Get it in writing before you pay.
