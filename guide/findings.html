Summary of findings

All 12 checklist items were reviewed line by line against official sources (FTC consumer pages, eCFR Title 16 Parts 23 and 429, NIST Office of Weights and Measures, LBMA, Jewelers of America).

Finding Detail Status
1. Karat definitions confirmed 10K = 41.7% (417), 14K = 58.3% (585), 18K = 75.0% (750), 24K = 99.99% (999). Sources: FTC + Jewelers of America. verified
2. "Solid gold" meaning confirmed Means the item is not hollow; must also carry a karat mark. Hollow items may not be marked "solid gold." Source: FTC. verified
3. Hallmark rule confirmed Karat mark plus maker's/trademark mark required by FTC guidance and National Stamping Act (15 U.S.C. 297). verified
4. Stamp alone is not proof A 14K/18K stamp by itself does not prove purity; no mandatory government assay office in the U.S. verified (as limitation)
5. Weight conversion confirmed 1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 grams exactly (NIST). No specific ring weights listed (none verified, none invented). verified
6. Seller reputation method confirmed FTC method: search seller name + "complaint"/"review", state attorney general, personal recommendations. No government jeweler database exists. verified
7. Price benchmark confirmed LBMA Gold Price = benchmark, twice daily in USD per troy ounce, 995 fineness. No live spot price claimed; no product prices listed. verified (method)
8. Return policy limits confirmed No general federal return right for online sales; FTC Cooling-Off Rule covers door-to-door/temporary-location sales only. Get return policy in writing. verified
9. Credit-card protection confirmed FCBA: 60-day dispute window, 30-day acknowledgment, 90-day resolution, $50 unauthorized-charge limit. Quality disputes not covered. verified
10. Documentation items confirmed Keep listing, receipt, description, seller's purity claims; get return policy in writing. verified
11. Independent jeweler / XRF not codified Sound consumer guidance (FTC points to jewelers via JVC; JA directory). XRF specifics come from industry/vendor sources, not government. unverified (guidance)
12. Walk-away rule confirmed If the seller won't allow independent verification or return, don't buy (FTC guidance). verified (guidance)

Overall conclusion

The checklist is consistent with official FTC guidance and the eCFR jewelry guides. The safest buying approach confirmed by the sources: reputable seller + proper markings (karat mark plus maker's mark) + reasonable price compared with gold weight and current gold value + credit-card payment + written return policy + documentation + independent jeweler/XRF verification after delivery.

Items needing your review: Two checklist items (independent jeweler verification; XRF testing) could not be confirmed from an official government source and are flagged as unverified guidance. See Flagged for Review for details, plus this site's known limitation that U.S. hallmarking is not government assay verification.
