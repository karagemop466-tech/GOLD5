## Verified facts

| Item | Verified value | Source |
|------|----------------|--------|
| 1 troy ounce (oz t) | Exactly 31.1034768 grams (exact conversion factor) | NIST OWM; LBMA |
| 1 gram | 0.03215075 troy ounces | NIST OWM |
| 1 pennyweight (dwt) | 1.55517384 grams = 24 grains = 0.05 troy oz | NIST OWM |
| 1 troy ounce | 20 pennyweights = 480 grains | NIST OWM |
| Weighing advice | A legitimate seller should be able to provide the ring's weight in grams | Checklist requirement (consumer guidance) |

## Calculation basis used on the site

Gold value of a ring = (weight in grams) × (karat ÷ 24) × (price per gram of pure gold), where
price per gram of pure gold = spot price per troy ounce ÷ 31.1034768.
