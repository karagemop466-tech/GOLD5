## Verified facts

| Item | Verified guidance | Source | Official? |
|------|-------------------|--------|-----------|
| Independent jeweler check | Take the ring to an independent jeweler before wearing/resizing to verify purity and weight | Checklist requirement (consumer guidance) | No — guidance |
| Find a jeweler | Jewelers of America operates a "Find a Jewelers of America jeweler" directory | Jewelers of America | Trade association |
| JVC mediation | Jewelers Vigilance Committee mediation program for disputes with jewelers | FTC consumer page | Government-linked NGO program |
| XRF testing | X-ray fluorescence is a non-destructive method to verify gold purity/composition; used by jewelers, pawn shops, refiners, assay offices | Thermo Fisher; industry sources | No — industry/vendor |
| XRF limitation | XRF reads surface composition (approx. outer microns); plating beneath surface may not be detected | Industry sources | No — industry |

## Irregularities / flags (must be on Flagged for Review page)

- **No government (federal/state) source was found mandating or describing XRF testing for consumers.** The XRF content comes from industry/vendor sources (instrument manufacturers, assay office coverage). It is presented as consumer guidance, not as a regulatory requirement, and is flagged here.
