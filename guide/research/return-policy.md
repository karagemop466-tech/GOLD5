## Verified facts

| Item | Verified guidance | Source |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| Get return/refund policy in writing | Find the store's refund and return policies — and get them in writing before you buy | FTC consumer page |
| Cooling-Off Rule scope | 3 days to cancel certain sales made at home, workplace, dorm, or seller's temporary location (hotel, convention center, fairground, restaurant) | FTC consumer page; 16 CFR Part 429 |
| Cooling-Off Rule does NOT cover | Regular retail store purchases or most online purchases (per FTC summaries) | FTC consumer page; 16 CFR Part 429 scope |
| Cancel deadline | Until midnight of the third business day after the sale | FTC consumer page |
| Seller obligations on cancel | Refund all payments within 10 business days, return trade-ins, cancel negotiable instruments | FTC consumer page; 16 CFR Part 429 |
| Custom/resized rings | Check specifically for exclusions on resized/custom rings (checklist requirement — seller policy dependent, no federal rule found) | Checklist requirement |

## Hallucination check

- No universal "right to return" was claimed. The FTC Cooling-Off Rule is narrowly scoped (door-to-door/temporary locations); most online jewelry sales are not covered.
- No specific seller return policies were invented.

## Irregularities / flags

- "Return after receiving and inspecting it" depends entirely on the seller's written return policy; there is no general federal return right for online jewelry purchases. Buyers must check for exclusions on resized/custom rings at the seller level. Flagged as practical limitation.
