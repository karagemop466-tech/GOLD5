## Verified facts

| Item | Verified value | Source |
|------|----------------|--------|
| Pure gold definition | 24 karat (24K) is pure gold; soft, alloyed for jewelry | FTC consumer page |
| Karat system basis | Total of pure gold + other metal = 24 parts | FTC consumer page |
| 18K gold | 18 parts gold + 6 parts other metal (75% pure gold) | FTC consumer page; Jewelers of America |
| 14K gold | 14 parts gold + 10 parts other metal (58.3% pure gold) | FTC consumer page; Jewelers of America |
| 10K gold | 41.7% pure gold (417 fineness) | Jewelers of America quality marks table |
| 14K mark | 14K; European mark 585; 58.3% pure gold | Jewelers of America |
| 18K mark | 18K; European mark 750; 75.0% pure gold | Jewelers of America |
| 24K mark | 24K; European mark 999; 99.99% pure gold | Jewelers of America |
| It is unfair/deceptive to misrepresent karat fineness | 16 CFR § 23.3(a) | eCFR |
| "Solid gold" meaning | Item is not hollow; also carries a karat mark (e.g., 14K, 18K) | FTC consumer page |
