## Verified facts

| Item | Verified guidance | Source |
|------|-------------------|--------|
| Preferred payment | Credit card, for purchase protection (checklist requirement) | Checklist requirement |
| Avoid irreversible payment methods | Checklist requirement (wire transfer, etc.) | Checklist requirement |
| Federal law | Fair Credit Billing Act (FCBA) sets dispute process for credit cards and open-end credit | FTC consumer page |
| Dispute window | Written dispute must reach issuer within 60 days of the first bill with the error | FTC consumer page |
| Issuer response | Acknowledge in writing within 30 days; resolve within 2 billing cycles, max 90 days | FTC consumer page |
| Unauthorized charges | Federal law limits responsibility to $50 (many issuers offer zero liability) | FTC consumer page |
| Billing errors covered | Charges for items not accepted/not delivered as agreed, wrong amount, unauthorized, etc. | FTC consumer page |
| Quality disputes | Disputes about the QUALITY of the item are NOT billing errors under FCBA | FTC consumer page |
| Payment method protection hierarchy | Credit cards have stronger legal protections than debit cards | FTC consumer page |

## Hallucination check

- No specific card networks, issuers, or zero-liability promises beyond the FTC's general statement were attributed to specific companies.
- The $50 figure is the FTC's stated federal limit for unauthorized charges.

## Irregularities / flags

None. All payment-related claims cite the FTC.
