Item Verified fact Source
Benchmark LBMA Gold Price — the internationally recognised benchmark price for gold delivered in London. LBMA — Gold Price
Set Twice daily (10:30 and 15:00 London time) in U.S. dollars per troy ounce; administered by ICE Benchmark Administration (IBA). LBMA — About Daily Auction Prices

Unit conversion 1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 grams (exact). NIST OWM

Calculator — gold value of a ring

Enter the ring's weight in grams, its karat, and today's spot price per troy ounce. Nothing is pre-filled: every number you enter is yours. Results are computed client-side; nothing is sent anywhere.

Formula: fine gold weight (g) = weight × (karat ÷ 24). Gold value = fine gold weight × (spot price ÷ 31.1034768). Karat percentages from FTC/Jewelers of America; conversion from NIST.
