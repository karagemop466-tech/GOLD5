About this project

GOLD5 collects, organizes, and analyzes the steps for buying a gold engagement ring online — 10K, 14K, 18K, and higher — so a buyer can verify purity, weight, seller reputation, price, return policy, payment protection, documentation, and independent testing before and after purchase.

The key rule, from the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC): a 14K or 18K stamp by itself does not prove the ring is actually that purity. The safest approach is a reputable seller + proper markings (karat mark plus maker's/trademark mark) + a reasonable price + independent testing after delivery.

1. Confirm the karat

10K, 14K, 18K, etc. Higher karat = more gold. View purity table →

2. Confirm solid gold

Listing must say solid gold — not plated, vermeil, gold-filled, gold overlay, or gold-tone. View rules →

3. Check the hallmark

10K/14K/18K stamp plus a maker's/manufacturer's mark. View rules →

4. Check the weight

Ring weight in grams. 1 troy ounce = 31.1034768 grams (NIST). View conversion →

5. Research the seller

Independent reviews and complaints; real contact information. View guidance →

6. Compare the price

Price vs. gold weight and current gold value. Cheap = red flag. Calculator →

7. Return policy

Return after receipt and inspection; check exclusions on resized/custom rings. View guidance →

8. Payment

Prefer a credit card for purchase protection. Avoid irreversible methods. View rights →

9. Documentation

Keep listing, receipt, description, and the seller's purity claims. View list →

10. Verify on arrival

Independent jeweler before wearing/resizing; ask for XRF testing. View guidance →
