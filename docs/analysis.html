Findings

  • No listing satisfied the full checklist on its page alone. Not one states a hallmark plus maker’s mark, and only 2 of 11 say “solid gold”. For every ring these are questions to put to the seller in writing before buying.
  • Weight is a major gap. 5 of 11 listings give no weight in grams, so gold content cannot be checked against price.
  • Among the 3 setting-only rings with a stated weight, price is 2.16× to 2.87× the calculated gold value (calculated).
  • Of the 5 rings with price, weight and karat (R06, R07, R08, R09, R10), 0 priced below the calculated gold value — so the “unusually cheap vs. gold weight” red flag was not triggered here. That does not prove purity: a stamp or a sensible price never does; only independent XRF testing does.
  • Return policies differ on resizing. Blue Nile and Allurez both make resized/altered items non-returnable; Blue Nile includes one free resize within 6 months. Do not resize before your independent jeweler has tested the ring. Details on Sellers.
  • Marketing price claims are not evidence. Allurez (“Everyone Else” price) and The Jewelry Master (“Retail Price”) show comparison prices. They are excluded from all calculations — see F-ALLUREZ-REF and F-JM-RETAIL.
  • Gold is volatile. Spot moves continuously; other sites’ quotes from earlier on 2026-10-22 (unverified snippets) are listed under G-SPOT-XCHECK. Every “calculated” number here scales with the Kitco snapshot used ($4,025.00/oz) — use the calculator for a fresh spot.

Recommended next steps

  1. Run the hallucination check again (python3 tools/check_hallucinations.py) and read Flagged for Review.
  2. For any ring you like: get written weight in grams, “solid gold” confirmation, photos of the karat + maker’s stamps, and the full return policy including resize/custom exclusions.
  3. Re-check the price and the gold spot on the day you buy; pay by credit card.
  4. On arrival and before wearing or resizing: independent jeweler — weigh it and run XRF; keep the listing, receipt and seller claims (see Checklist).
