## 4. Hallucination hunt (the point of the pass)

Checked deliberately, in this order:

1. **Every rendered `$` against the dataset allow-list** — `verify.py` C8 (233 allowed values) and
   `independent_audit.py` A6. Both green. C8 *did* catch two of my own errors during the pass: a stale

2. **Hand-written dollar figures inside notes and flags** — extracted with a script and compared one by

   what Brilliant Earth displayed; **zero conversions**. The mixed-currency BE page is the strongest
   reason to keep that rule.
4. **Derived arithmetic** — C4 (spot×troy, purity rows, fine gold, metal value) and the independent
   audit's A1–A5, A2b (60 raw-gold cells) all recompute from raw inputs.
5. **Dates** — every row's `checkedOn` is 2026-10-23 and C16 rejects a future or pass-date prior row.
   The 2026-10-22 archive table on the Verification page now says what it is: history, not current data.
6. **Counts in prose** — the homepage, README and dashboard counts are computed from `meta.coverage` and
   `len(...)`, not typed. The README's "7 have no verified return policy" was checked against
   `returnsVerified` (18 of 25 verified) and is correct.

**Recommendation for the next reviewer:** hunt in the same order, and add one check to the suite —
"no dollar figure may appear in a flag or note unless it also appears in an evidence file", which is what
`independent_audit.py` A9 does for prices. A9 is the check that would have caught the `$529` automatically.
