Gold Weight & Value Calculator

Check what the gold in a ring is worth before you pay.

◆ NO HALLUCINATIONS RULE
Every price, weight, karat and policy on this site was copied from the linked official seller page on the capture date (2026-10-22). Anything that could not be confirmed there is left blank and flagged unverified — nothing is guessed, estimated or inferred. Values labelled calculated are plain arithmetic on verified inputs, with the formula shown. Prices change and gold moves every few seconds: re-check the listing before you buy. A hallucination check runs after every review pass — and you should run it again too (python3 tools/check_hallucinations.py).

Gold content and melt value of a ring: weight (g) × (spot per troy oz ÷ 31.1034768 g) × karat ÷ 24. The spot field starts at the Kitco snapshot below (2026-10-22, Oct 22, 2026 - 19:11 NY Time (page footnote: as of October 22, 2026 at 7:10 p.m. EDT)). Anything you type is your own input, not verified site data. For a newer spot price use kitco.com.
