What the hallucination check enforces

  1. C1 every ring row links to an official page on its own seller’s domain;
  2. C2 every saved evidence excerpt records its source URL and capture date (stale after 7 days = warning);
  3. C3 every price, karat, weight and “solid” claim appears verbatim in the saved evidence — an invented number fails the build;
  4. C4 every blank value carries an “unverified” flag — nothing is filled in;
  5. C5 the spot price and Kitco purity table match the saved Kitco text and k/24 arithmetic;
  6. C6 seller contacts appear in evidence;
  7. C7 every $ amount printed on every page is either in evidence or a registered calculation — typed-in numbers fail;
  8. C8 all internal links and anchors resolve, and every external link is a known source URL;
  9. C9 sanity checks: below-melt prices, implausible weights, duplicate URLs, identical specs across listings.

Recommendation: run it after every review or data change: python3 tools/check_hallucinations.py --log "what I reviewed", then rebuild with python3 tools/build_site.py. A passing check shows internal consistency with the saved excerpts; it cannot prove the seller’s page is truthful, so also use the source links and the in-person XRF test.

Limit: the check cannot re-fetch seller sites from the build sandbox. Live-page verification was done at capture time; re-run the capture before relying on a price.
