⚠ No hallucinations — that is the rule of this project

Every price, weight, and claim on this site was read from the linked official source page on the check date and is logged in Sources. Values we could not confirm from an official source are left blank and published on the Flagged for Review page — they are never inferred or filled in. Derived numbers (gold content, metal value) are computed live in your browser from raw inputs. After every review, re-run the hallucination/security pass: python3 scripts/verify.py.
