How the price-vs-gold test works

1. Get the ring's weight in grams (ask the seller — a legitimate seller can provide it).
2. Pure gold grams = weight × karat/24 (14K → ×0.5833, 18K → ×0.75).
3. Metal value = pure gold grams × spot $/g (see table above, re-verified the day of purchase).
4. Compare the price: below metal value = red flag (nobody sells solid gold below melt); a healthy engagement ring typically prices at 2–5× metal value to cover the stone, labor, and margin.
5. Try it with any listing on the calculator.
