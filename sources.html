How evidence is classified

Seller-stated: a seller's own page states it; this is not independent confirmation. Unverified — blank: the evidence checked does not establish a value. Flagged: one or more of the buyer's requirements or critical documentation is still missing.

Checklist vs. legal guidance

The demand for explicit solid-gold language, a hallmark plus maker's mark, weight in grams, inspection returns, and post-delivery testing is this project's buyer-protection screening standard. It is not represented here as a complete statement of legal requirements. The cited eCFR Jewelry Guides address advertising representations; they cannot test a specific ring.
