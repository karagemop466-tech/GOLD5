high The seller behind four of the best-scoring rows has 49 BBB complaints in three years

Allurez is BBB-accredited (A+, since 2/24/2011), and its BBB complaints page shows 49 complaints in the last 3 years and 8 in the last 12 months: 23 product issues, 13 service or repair, 7 delivery, 3 order, 2 sales and advertising, 1 customer service; 40 answered and 9 resolved. One displayed complaint describes a ring whose colour did not match the photo; the buyer was refunded after BBB involvement, less a shipping charge the business later refunded as a goodwill gesture. A published review also says the business asked the reviewer to remove a negative review to get the ring back. None of this proves any particular ring is defective — it does change how much the 4-of-6 score on A01-A04 should be leaned on, and it is why the exchange-only policy on 'Design Your Own' rings matters more here.

Affects: A01, A02, A03, A04

high No listing shows a hallmark or maker's mark

Not one of the 27 listings shows a stamp photo or states a hallmark plus maker's mark. The FTC tells buyers not to buy gold jewellery that lacks a trademark beside the karat mark, and 16 CFR 23.10 note 2 (National Stamping Act, 15 U.S.C. 297) requires the maker's name/trademark with a quality mark. This is the single biggest gap in this project: it can only be closed physically, by an independent jeweler inspecting the stamp.

Affects: A01, A02, A03, A04, A05, A06, A07, A08, A09, A10, A12, B01, B02, B03, B04, B05, B06, B07, B08, B09, B10, B11, B12, B13, B14, B15

high Most sellers never state 'solid gold' on the listing

Only 4 of 27 rows contain a seller statement that the metal is solid (Avianne title, Jewelry Master description, the VeraByEden FAQ, and two Etsy listing descriptions). The rest merely name the metal, which is not the same claim — the FTC guide defines 'solid gold' as gold that is not hollow, and 16 CFR 23.3 forbids calling a hollow item 'solid'. Blank + flagged.
