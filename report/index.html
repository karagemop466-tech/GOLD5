The rule this project runs on: no hallucinations

Every price, weight, karat, hallmark claim and return term on this site was read from the seller's own page on the date shown, and the exact text that was on the page is stored in verification/evidence/. If a value could not be confirmed, the cell is blank and the row carries a flag — never an estimate, never a "typical" number, never a currency conversion.

A checker enforces it: python3 tools/check_report.py re-finds every recorded value in the saved page text, re-derives every calculated number, and fails the build on any amount that is not traceable. It is run after every review, and the result is published on the Method page.

What we found, in one paragraph

Of 27 gold ring listings examined, none satisfies every requirement in the buying checklist — the best row clears 4 of the 6 machine-checkable tests. The blocking gaps are consistent across every seller: not one listing states a hallmark together with a maker's mark, and 19 of 27 publish no gram weight, so the price-versus-gold-value test cannot even be run from the page. Only 2 listings state "solid gold" and publish both a weight and a price.

Read the top rows with the seller check beside them

The four highest-scoring rows (A01–A04) are Allurez settings. Allurez is BBB-accredited, A+, since 2/24/2011 — and its BBB complaints page also shows 49 complaints in the last 3 years and 8 in the last 12 months (23 product issues, 13 service or repair, 7 delivery). Its "Design Your Own" engagement rings are exchange-only, so a refund is not the remedy if the ring is not what you expected. That is why the score reads the listing, not the seller: G-ALLUREZ-COMPLAINTS · G-EXCHANGE-ONLY.

"Price ÷ metal value" is arithmetic, not a verdict: it compares the asking price with the gold content alone. Stones, labour and brand sit on top of that, so a high multiple is normal for a diamond ring and is not evidence of overcharging. The direction that matters for fraud is the other one — a price below the metal value. No row in this catalogue is below it.
