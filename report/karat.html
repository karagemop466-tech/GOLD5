What the karat comparison shows — and what it does not

Allurez sells the same filigree design in 14K (style U5882) and 18K (style U5885). The 14K page lists 5.00 g at $1,085; the 18K page lists 5.90 g at $1,316. That is +$231 for +0.90 g of metal and a higher karat, i.e. most of the difference is accounted for by the extra gold, not by a karat premium. But note the style numbers differ, so this is a same-family comparison, not the same SKU in two metals — recorded as G-18K-DIFFERENT-SKU.

The Brilliant Earth Freesia is the one true same-SKU pair (BE1343-14KY / BE1343-18KY), but its 18K page was served in euros at capture, so no USD comparison is published here. Converting it would be an inference.
