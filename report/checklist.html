Bottom line

Nothing in this catalogue is buyable on the strength of its listing alone. The listing can tell you the karat, sometimes the weight, and the return terms. It cannot tell you whether the ring is solid, whether it is hallmarked, or whether the gold is the karat claimed — those need the physical ring, a jeweler, and an XRF reading. What this catalogue does is remove the guesswork from everything that can be read, and mark the rest clearly.
