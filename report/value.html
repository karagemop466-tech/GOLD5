Formulas. Fine gold (g) = weight (g) × karat ÷ 24. Metal value (USD) = fine gold (g) × $129.47/g. Troy ounces = weight ÷ 31.1034768. Every number in this table is recomputed by the checker, never typed.

Metal value is not what the ring is worth: it is the gold content valued at spot, before stones, making charges, hallmarking, retailer margin and tax. It is the floor that lets you see whether a price is absurd.

Worked example, from the catalogue

Allurez U5882 publishes 5.00 g (approximate) of 14K at $1,085.

  1. Fine gold = 5.00 × 14 ÷ 24 = 2.9167 g
  2. Metal value = 2.9167 g × $129.47 = $377.62
  3. Price ÷ metal value = 1085 ÷ $377.62 = 2.87×
  4. So $707.38 of the price is not gold — diamond-free setting, labour, margin. Whether that is fair is a judgement; the arithmetic is not.
