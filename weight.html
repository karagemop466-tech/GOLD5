Troy ounce to grams (official conversion)

Precious metals are weighed in troy ounces, not the everyday (avoirdupois) ounce used on food labels. Confusing the two introduces roughly a 10% error.

Unit Grams (exact) Source
1 troy ounce (oz t) 31.1034768 g NIST Office of Weights and Measures — Conversion Factors for Precious Metal Sales
1 gram (g) 0.03215075 troy oz NIST OWM
1 pennyweight (dwt) 1.55517384 g (24 grains = 0.05 troy oz) NIST OWM
1 troy ounce 20 pennyweights = 480 grains NIST OWM

How to check a ring's weight

  • A legitimate seller should be able to provide the ring's weight in grams.
  • Use the troy-ounce conversion (31.1034768 g = 1 oz t) when comparing a per-ounce gold price with a per-gram ring weight.
  • Independent verification: ask an independent jeweler to weigh the ring after delivery.

Pure gold content of a ring

Fine gold weight (grams) = ring weight (grams) × (karat ÷ 24).

Example (illustrative only, no product involved): a 5 g 14K ring contains 5 × (14/24) = 2.9167 g of pure gold. All karat percentages from FTC/Jewelers of America; conversion from NIST.

No specific ring weights are listed on this site. No products are reviewed or sold here; every weight figure would be seller-provided and must be verified independently.
