Upon Arrival

Before wearing or resizing the ring, take it to an independent jeweler and ask them to verify the gold purity and weight.

Step Action Source Verified Source Link
Independent jeweler verification Take the ring to an independent jeweler (not affiliated with the seller) Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Verify purity Ask the jeweler to verify the gold karat/purity matches the seller's claims Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Verify weight Ask the jeweler to verify the weight in grams matches the seller's claims Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Before wearing/resizing Complete verification before wearing or resizing the ring Verified FTC Consumer Guide

Why Independent Verification Matters

  • A karat stamp alone does not prove the ring is actually that purity
  • Independent testing can catch misrepresentation before it's too late to return
  • If purity or weight doesn't match the seller's claims, use your return policy
  • Document all findings for your records

Source: FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry
