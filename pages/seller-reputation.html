Seller Reputation

Research independent reviews and complaints. Verify the business has real contact information. Do not rely solely on what you see on the seller's own website or social media.

Check Action Source Verified Source Link
Independent reviews Type the seller's name + "complaint" or "review" into a search engine Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Personal recommendations Ask people you personally know for recommendations Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Real contact information Verify the business has a real address, phone number, and customer service contact Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Report problems Report to FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov, your state attorney general, or Jewelers Vigilance Committee (JVClegal.org) Verified FTC Consumer Guide

Red Flags

  • No verifiable physical address or phone number
  • Only social media presence, no independent reviews
  • Multiple complaints about non-delivery, misrepresentation, or refund issues
  • Seller refuses to provide documentation about gold purity

Source: FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry
