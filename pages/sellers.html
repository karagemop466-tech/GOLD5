What every one of these policies has in common

Each retailer excludes resized or altered rings, and most exclude engraved or personalised items, from returns. That directly changes your order of operations:

  1. Order the correct size the first time, and do not accept a free resize before inspecting.
  2. Inspect the stamp, weight and purity with an independent jeweler while the ring is still returnable.
  3. Keep the grading report — Blue Nile charges $150 to replace a missing diamond report.
  4. Pay by credit card so a misdescribed item can be disputed.

About independent reviews

This project links each seller's BBB search page rather than republishing star ratings. Ratings and complaint counts change daily, are not primary evidence about a specific ring, and copying one would violate the no-hallucination rule as of a later reading. Open the links yourself and check the number of closed complaints, not just the letter grade.
