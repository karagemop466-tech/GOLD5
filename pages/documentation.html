Documentation

Keep the listing, receipt, description, and seller's claims about gold purity. This documentation is essential if you need to dispute the purchase or verify authenticity later.

Document Action Source Verified Source Link
Product listing Save a copy/screenshot of the full listing including all claims about gold purity Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Receipt Keep the receipt showing price, date, and seller information Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Product description Save the full product description including karat, weight, and material claims Verified FTC Consumer Guide
Seller's purity claims Document all seller claims about gold karat, solid gold status, and hallmarks Verified FTC Consumer Guide

Why Documentation Matters

  • Essential for disputing charges with your credit card company
  • Needed if you report the seller to the FTC or state attorney general
  • Required by independent jewelers for XRF testing verification
  • Compare seller's claims against independent test results after delivery

Source: FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry
