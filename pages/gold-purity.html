Gold Purity — Karat System

The karat mark tells you how much pure gold a piece contains. Pure gold (24K) is soft, so it is mixed with other metals for durability. The total of pure gold and other metal adds up to 24 parts.

Key FTC Guidance (16 CFR Part 23)

  • Karats are parts out of 24: 18K gold = 18 parts gold + 6 parts other metal. 14K = 14 parts gold + 10 parts other metal.
  • Maker's mark required: Near the karat quality mark, look for the name or U.S. registered trademark of the company that stands behind the mark. If you don't see a trademark with the karat mark, don't buy it.
  • Higher karat = more gold: 24K is pure gold; lower karats contain more alloy metals.
  • Source: FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry
