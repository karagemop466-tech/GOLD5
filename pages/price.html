How to Evaluate Price

  • Calculate the raw gold value: weight (grams) × karat purity fraction × current spot price per gram
  • Compare the asking price to the raw gold value
  • An unusually cheap price compared to gold weight is a major red flag
  • Retail price includes craftsmanship, design, brand, and markup — it will be above melt value
  • Never pay more than you can verify the gold is worth
