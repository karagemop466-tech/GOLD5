Payment

Prefer a credit card for purchase protection. Avoid irreversible payment methods.

Payment Method Recommendation Reason Source Verified Source Link
Credit card Preferred Purchase protection; can dispute charges Verified FTC Consumer Advice
Wire transfer Avoid Irreversible; no purchase protection Verified FTC Consumer Advice
Cryptocurrency Avoid Irreversible; no purchase protection Verified FTC Consumer Advice
Cash / money order Avoid Irreversible; no purchase protection Verified FTC Consumer Advice

Guidance

  • Use a credit card for purchase protection — you can dispute charges if something goes wrong
  • Avoid wire transfers, cryptocurrency, cash, or money orders — these are irreversible
  • If a seller insists on irreversible payment methods, that's a red flag
  • Keep records of all payment transactions

Source: FTC Consumer Advice — Paying for Purchases
