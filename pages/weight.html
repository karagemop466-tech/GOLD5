Weight — Grams

Check the ring's weight in grams. A legitimate seller should be able to provide it. Weight is essential for calculating the raw gold value.

Ring weight Unverified — Ask seller for weight in grams Unverified FTC — Before You Buy

What to Check

  • Ask the seller for the ring's weight in grams before purchasing
  • A legitimate seller should be able to provide the weight
  • Weight is needed to calculate raw gold value using the karat purity
  • After delivery, take to an independent jeweler to verify weight

Source: FTC — Buying Platinum, Gold, and Silver Jewelry
