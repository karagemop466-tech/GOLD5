Gold bands

Solid-gold rings with no stones — the cleanest test of metal value. A plain gold band strips the problem down to two numbers: grams and karat. It is the only category here where the asking price can be divided by the metal actually contained in the ring, because the seller publishes the weight.

Before you buy from this category

  • 2 of 3 rows here publish a gram weight — start with those, because the metal value can be checked against the price. For the rest, ask for the weight in writing.
  • Request a photo of the inside of the band showing the karat stamp and the maker's mark (F-005).
  • Confirm in writing that the metal is solid, not plated or vermeil (F-006).
  • Check the return exclusions for resized and personalised items on the Sellers page before you let anyone size it.
