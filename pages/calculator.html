Gold Calculator — Raw Gold Value

Calculate the raw gold melt value from the ring's weight, karat purity, and current gold spot price. Enter your own values — nothing is pre-filled to avoid hallucination.

How It Works

  • Pure gold weight = ring weight × (karat ÷ 24)
  • Raw gold value = pure gold weight × spot price per gram
  • This is the raw gold (melt) value only — it does not include craftsmanship, gemstones, brand premium, or retail markup
  • Purity fractions: 24K = 1.000, 18K = 0.750, 14K = 0.583, 10K = 0.417

Sources: FTC Consumer Guide · LBMA (spot price)
